Questions, answered.

Does it work on any job site?

The extension works on about 50 of the most used job sites in India, including LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and company career pages on Greenhouse, Lever and Workday. For any other site, paste the job description into the dashboard.

Is the Chrome extension on the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. Until it is, you can paste a job description into the dashboard and use everything else.

Will it make up skills or experience?

Never. It only reorders and highlights what is already in your resume. A new skill needs your confirmation first.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No. It prepares everything, but you always press apply yourself.

Does it change my resume design?

No. Your tailored resume is a copy in your own layout.

What does it cost?

Every account starts with 50 free credits and no card. One credit is one job. When they run out, buy a pack: 30 credits for ₹199, 100 for ₹499 or 300 for ₹1,299. It is a one-time payment, not a subscription, and credits never expire.
